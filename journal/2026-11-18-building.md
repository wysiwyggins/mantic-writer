# Building

Checked out the local hackerspace foulab last night, might be space there to work on the mantic reader installation. Took a trip to the wholsale electronics shop with Austin this morning and grabbed some assorted buttons and switches. Need a better potentiometer. I got an unusual momentary switch that turns like an ignition switch, its knob has a little arrow on it, nice and metal. I'm unsure what it could be for (maybe saving or activating the attract mode)

In my mind I am wishing I had a carnival love-tester style conductive switch. A metal palm-print to put your hand in or a metal pistol grip with something you squeeze or buttons for each finger. I'd like something nice to touch, and I don't know if I have the time and equipment to do that. Something found would be best. It's possible that there is something lurking in the bins at Foulab. For now I am going to use copper tape. Will solder stick to copper tape? The arduino nano is working with the rpi 5 now (I got a 'dumb' power supply for the Pi and a good usb a to c data cable for the arduino and now it's working.) The Pi is sending sound through hdmi and my monitor has no sound so I can't test that. I'm a little annoyed/surprised there's not a regular headphone jack on it. It has bluetooth but I really don't want to do bluetooth audio for an installation. I will just hope Radio Snack has a projector that goes out to some kind of audio. 

One issue, the Rpi is asking to unlock the default keyring every time it opens chromium. Searching I found a few reports of this, I'll fiddle with it.
