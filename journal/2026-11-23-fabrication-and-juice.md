I am also feeling like this isn't enough. Like it's not theatrical enough to be entertaining at a party. I played with Rachel's *Electronic Sweet N' Fun* NES rom for a while today and I feel like I need a transition or waiting screen, or some more theater to communicate what's happening, like a loader.

I could build off the idea that the contacts are love testing or whatever, measuring the body/character. I have the word list of "skills" from grotto and chiromancer that would make good "testing" words for a loading screen. I could also say something like "Your card is..." or actually, "Your talisman is..." before showing the figure. I also added a vertical meander and meander corner tile if I wanted to do some kind of screen wipe transition, have the meander wipe the screen in a big coil...

Here is an issue with a bunch of theater though. If we make people wait for a result longer, it's more dramatic, but if they are putting their hands and fingers on all the contacts each time and not changing the knob, then they are waiting a long time to get the same result.

I need to induce them to use the knob maybe? I was thinking maybe adding symbols on either side of the slider ui that shows up. I don't have a happy/sad face. Maybe I could put gender icons on either side for some kind of gender spectrum feeling thing?
