# Mantic Writer

Looking at one of the fragmentary processing sketches from "Didaktik Gama" to possibly develop for a show in a few weeks.

This used tracery and word lists from corpora project and my own libraries, and the spritesheet and sounds from Dungeon Mode Kit to simulate some sort of sigil/ideogram/divination figure from no known system.

Looking at this again I'm struggling to find what it is about, since it seems to come from an undefined fantasy world but uses characters from our world and has names written in english using words we know (It's from a dungeon world!)

This morning I thought that each glyph could be introduced one at a time as a form of writing, with the title on top a translation.

The glyphs could also be added in a bilaterally symmetrical shape, like [Technopaegnia](https://monoskop.org/Greece)... or would this create too much sameness while the current arrangements have a vast amount of variation but seem very random?

Or what if the sketch assigns each glyph to a key, the player types out each glyph and at some point it prints the 'meaning' phrase. The problem with this is that many of the glyphs are regular alphanumeric and we don't want them to just type out regular messages. it would be an unfamiliar input device, something really strange, like an arrangement of buttons on a rotary knob. But this means a bunch of building work and testing and I only have a couple of weeks.
