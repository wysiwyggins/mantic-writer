# planetary hours

Without a clock prop, I decided to add a clock to the UI, every hour the month, day and a symbol representing "planetary hour" (astrological sign)

To deal with the fact that the finger contacts won't have indicator lights or tactile clicks, I added some onscreen indicators to show when a contact is touched (I used the astrological symbols I found in palmistry books while researching Chiromancer.) I also added a potentiometer position indicator that shows up when there's an adjustment. I haven't tested it with a pot yet though, there may need to be debouncing etc to keep the indicator from always appearing.

I think the physical interface is gonna be rough, probably a board with a pot and some contacts. I am not going to bother with the saving signs and attract mode in this version.
