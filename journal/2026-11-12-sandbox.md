# Sandbox

I had a very nice workshop session with the Concordia TAG Sandbox group today. Some takeaways—

The look and feel and text all seem to be working, it's displaced in time, has a bit of mystery to it.

The hover to see individual glyph names interaction isn't important to keep in a installation version. Knowing the individual glyph names distracts from the work your brain does to try to understand how they form the overarching concept.

Some desirable directions could be-

a complex multi key input like a keyboard etc that could provide the seed for the sound/color/figures/name instead of a single trigger or typing on each figure with a keyboard, like playing a chord

another conceptual level where whole cards are saved and can be replayed. Maybe an attract mode replays saved cards

Austin says he has an arduino nano esp32, which has some touch capacitive pins. That might be a great way to have an unsual input that generates seed for the figures rather than just act as a momentary button trigger.
